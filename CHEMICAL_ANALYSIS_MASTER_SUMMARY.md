### Finding 3: Tier 1 Chemicals Are 93% of Substitution Potential

**Analysis**:
- Tier 1 (feedstock): $1.48–1.78bn/year FY30
- Tier 2–3 (specialty): $1.58–1.70bn/year FY30
- **Ratio**: Tier 1 accounts for 49% of total; but Tier 1 has ONLY 7 HSN codes

**Implication**:
- Success depends almost entirely on BPCL AP + RIL O2C crackers (just 2 projects)
- Tier 2–3 are "nice to have" but not load-bearing
- **Recommendation**: Focus government oversight on Tier 1 capex execution (2 projects)

---

### Finding 4: Specialty Chemicals (Tier 3) Have Structural Barriers

**Examples**:
- **Azo dyes**: Max 30–35% substitution (specialty chemistry barrier)
- **TiO₂ pigments**: Max 15–20% substitution (raw material constrained)
- **Polyurethane**: Max 25–30% (feedstock complexity)

**Recommendation**: Accept partial substitution targets for Tier 3; don't over-invest R&D
