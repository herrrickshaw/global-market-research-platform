## KEY NEWS SOURCES TO MONITOR

### Tier 1: Official Government Sources
1. **PIB (Press Information Bureau)** — https://pib.gov.in
   - Search: "chemical", "anti-dumping", "DGFT", "import substitution"
   - Updates: Daily (official announcements from ministries)

2. **Ministry of Chemicals & Petrochemicals** — https://chemicals.gov.in
   - Monthly reports, PLI scheme updates, capex tracking
   - Updates: Monthly

3. **DGFT (Directorate General of Foreign Trade)** — https://dgft.gov.in
   - Anti-dumping investigations, duty notifications
   - Updates: Bi-weekly (case decisions)

4. **Stock Exchange Announcements** (BSE/NSE)
   - RIL, BPCL, HPCL, Jindal, Adani capex announcements
   - Search: Company name + "capex" or "project update"

### Tier 2: Industry & Business News
1. **The Hindu Business Line** — https://www.thehindubusinessline.com
   - Chemicals & Petrochemicals section
   - Updates: Daily

2. **Business Today** — https://www.businesstoday.in
   - Industry reports, company announcements
   - Updates: Daily

3. **Economic Times** — https://economictimes.indiatimes.com
   - Chemicals, Oil & Gas, Policy updates
   - Updates: Daily

4. **Petroleum & Energy Intelligence** — ICIS, Chemical & Engineering News
   - Subscribed feeds (if available)
   - Updates: Weekly

### Tier 3: Sector-Specific Sources
1. **FICCI (Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce & Industry)**
   - Chemical sector briefings, industry data
   - Updates: Monthly/Quarterly

2. **ASSOCHAM (Associated Chambers of Commerce)**
   - Industry surveys, capex tracking
   - Updates: Quarterly

3. **CareEdge, ICRA, CRISIL** — Rating agency reports
   - Industry analysis, company financials
   - Updates: Quarterly
