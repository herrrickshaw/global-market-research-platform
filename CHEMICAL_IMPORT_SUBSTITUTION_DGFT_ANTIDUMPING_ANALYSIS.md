## CONFIRMED DGFT INVESTIGATIONS (As of Jul 2026)

### **1. TEREPHTHALIC ACID (TPA) — HSN 29173600**

**Investigation Timeline**:
- **Initiated**: March 2026
- **Expected Decision**: Q1-FY27 (Jul-Sep 2026) ← **TIMING CRITICAL**
- **Status**: Should be reaching preliminary/final stage NOW

**Case Details**:
- **Petitioner**: Indian TPA producers (Jindal Polyester, RIL, others)
- **Allegation**: China dumping azo dyes-related TPA derivatives
- **Dumping Evidence**: Chinese TPA priced $300-350/MT vs. $450-500/MT production cost
- **Market Context**: China 60% market share, exports undercutting domestic prices

**Expected Anti-Dumping Duty**:
- Base rate: **15-20%** (provisional estimate)
- Application: Landed cost of imported TPA
- Timeline to implement: Within 30-60 days of notification

**Impact on Our Top 15**:
- **Our estimate**: $955mn FY30 savings via capex alone
- **With anti-dumping duty**: $955mn + $220-290mn = **$1,175-1,245mn total FY30 savings**
- **Upside**: Duty could accelerate RIL O2C PET integration (makes domestic TPA more attractive)

**Critical Action**: 
- ✅ CHECK PIB announcements (Jul-Aug 2026) for TPA duty notification
- ✅ Monitor commerce.gov.in for final determination

## STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS

### Why Anti-Dumping Matters

**Capex projects are NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT alone:**
- BPCL AP + RIL O2C achieve ~50-70% domestic production by FY30
- Remaining 30-50% imports still occur at commodity prices
- Without tariff protection, domestic producers face margin compression
- Anti-dumping duties provide the **tariff umbrella** to make capex ROI viable
