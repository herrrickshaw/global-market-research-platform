   Results by FY30:
   • Polypropylene: Zero import dependency (capacity 2.3→4.1 MMTPA)
   • Polyethylene: 70-80% import substitution (2.1→4.2 MMTPA)
   • Polyester: 30-40% domestic sourcing
   • Export competitiveness: +2-4% cost advantage vs Bangladesh/Vietnam

   Segment Savings:

   └─ Specialty: ₹13,280 cr (Diversified segments)

2. ETHANOL BLENDING & PETROL EXPORT —————————— ₹12,000–18,000 cr/year

   Mechanism: Ethanol displaces domestic petrol (E20→E30 ramp FY26-FY30)
   Vehicles buy more litres for same km → freed petrol exports

   FY30 Benefits (E27 scenario, recommended):
   ├─ Freed petrol export value:      ₹62,300 cr ($7.3bn)
   ├─ OMC pump margin (extra volume): ₹1,000 cr
   ├─ Tax revenue (excise + VAT):     ₹11,500 cr
   └─ State SGST (5%, price-neutral): ₹4,787 cr ← NEW POLICY LEVER
   ────────────────────────
   Subtotal:    ₹79,587 cr/year total (₹12-18k cr/yr average FY26-30)

   Supply: 4,530 cr L/year capacity exists (overcapacity)
   Constraint: Feedstock availability (maize, FCI rice, molasses)

3. RENEWABLE FUELS & GAS SUBSTITUTION ———————— ₹10,000–14,000 cr/year

   CBG (Compressed Biogas) via SATAT: 5 MMTPA by FY30
   Displaces LNG imports (₹80,000 cr/year market)

   Benefits:
   ├─ LNG import substitution (10-15%):       ₹8,000–12,000 cr/year
   ├─ Bio-isobutanol (SAF route, niche):      ₹2,000–3,000 cr/year
   └─ Distillery conversion (grain ethanol):  ₹500–1,000 cr/year
   ────────────────────────
   Subtotal:    ₹10,000–14,000 cr/year

TOP 5 RISKS & MITIGATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

→ MITIGATION: Allocate ₹5,000 cr risk capital; phase capex; fast-track approvals

→ MITIGATION: Monitor 2027-28; anti-dumping duties if domestic capacity idles

RISK 5: State government revenue resistance (SGST coordination)
→ MITIGATION: Price-neutral design (₹4,787 cr new state revenue, no consumer impact)
