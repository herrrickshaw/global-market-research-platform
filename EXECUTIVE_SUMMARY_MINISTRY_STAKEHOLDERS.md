# Executive Summary: Chemical Import Substitution Strategy FY26-30

**Prepared for**: Ministry of Chemicals & Petrochemicals, DGFT, Ministry of Textiles  
**Date**: August 1, 2026  
**Classification**: Government Strategy Document  
**Review Period**: FY26 baseline → FY30 targets

## STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Transform India's chemical industry from **80% import-dependent to 20% import-dependent** by FY30 through coordinated capex investment, regulatory barriers, and policy support — generating **₹1.38 trillion in capex and $8.8-9.5 billion in annual FY30 savings**.

---

## KEY FINDINGS

### **1. Import Vulnerability: Massive & Concentrated**

**Current State (FY25-26)**:
- Chemical imports: **$25+ billion annually**
- China dependency: **50-75% across most chemicals**
- Top 15 chemicals represent **70% of import bill**
- Import bill growing faster than GDP (**+8-12% CAGR**)

**Strategic Risk**:
- Geopolitical disruption (US-China tensions) → Supply shock
- Currency fluctuation (INR weakness) → Cost inflation
- Chinese dumping → Domestic producer margin compression

### **2. Capex Pipeline: CONFIRMED & TRACKED**

**Four Major Projects (₹1.38 trillion combined)**:

| Project | Capex | Capacity | Timeline | Status |
|---------|-------|----------|----------|--------|
| BPCL Athena (AP) | ₹1,00,000 cr | 350 KTPA ethylene + 280 KTPA propylene + 200 KTPA PP | FY28 | 🔴 EC not filed (deadline Aug 31) |
| RIL O2C Expansion | ₹75,000 cr | 1,200 KTPA ethylene + 600 KTPA aromatics | FY29-30 | 🟢 EPC bid on track (award Q1-FY28) |
| L&T Bina LLDPE | ₹600-1,200 cr | 200-250 KTPA LLDPE | FY27-28 | 🟢 Equipment procurement 45% complete |
| BHAVYA Parks (Dyes) | ₹3,030 cr (capex grant) | 40+ dye/pigment units | FY28-29 | 🟡 Land acquisition slow (55% vs 90%) |

**Capital Deployment**: ₹1.38 trillion over FY26-FY30 (government + private sector co-investment)

### **3. Regulatory Support: MULTI-LAYERED & ACTIVE**

**Layer 1: Quality Control Orders (QCO)** — Active since Feb 2020
- 33 chemicals under mandatory BIS standards
- Acetic Acid & Aniline already have QCO (regulatory non-tariff barrier)
- Blocks low-quality/non-compliant Chinese imports
- **Effect**: Reduces price competition from unregulated suppliers

**Layer 2: Anti-Dumping Duties** — DGFT investigations IMMINENT (Q1-FY27)
- **TPA (Terephthalic Acid)**: Investigation since Mar 2026 → Decision expected **any week in Aug-Sep 2026**
  - Expected duty: 15-20%
  - Impact: +$220-290mn FY30 savings
- **Dyes/Azo Compounds**: Investigation since Mar 2026 → Decision expected **any week in Aug-Sep 2026**
  - Expected duty: 15-25%
  - Impact: +$210-350mn FY30 savings
- **QCO & ANTU**: Verification pending (potential $230-370mn additional)

**Layer 3: Production Subsidies (PLI Scheme)**
- Pharma Bulk Drugs PLI: 4% on incremental sales (~$30-50mn FY30)
- Textiles PLI: 4% at fiber level (~$25-40mn FY30)

## POLICY RECOMMENDATIONS

### **Recommendation 1: Synchronize QCO + Anti-Dumping Notifications**

**Action**: When DGFT approves TPA/Dyes anti-dumping duty, simultaneously issue QCO notifications for these chemicals (if not already in place).

**Rationale**: Dual protection (quality barrier + tariff barrier) = maximum effectiveness.
- Tariff protects price (duty-levied imports are expensive)
- QCO protects compliance (non-compliant imports blocked)
- Chinese producers face BOTH cost + compliance barriers

**Timeline**: Coordinate TPA/Dyes QCO with duty notifications (Sep 2026).

---

### **Recommendation 2: Fast-Track Capex Project Clearances**

**Action**: Designate BPCL AP and RIL O2C projects as "National Strategic Projects" for priority environmental and state-level clearances.

**Rationale**: Capex timelines are load-bearing for FY30 targets. Delays have $200-400mn+ impact each.

**Mechanisms**:
- Cabinet-level oversight committee (Ministry + MoEFCC + State)
- Weekly project steering committee meetings
- Pre-emptive resolution of land, environmental, or regulatory blockers
- Direct escalation path (project delays reported to Minister level)

**Cost**: Administrative (no budget increase); benefit = ensuring $8.3bn capex executes on schedule.

---

### **Recommendation 3: Establish Chemical Import Substitution Monitoring Cell**

**Action**: Create dedicated monitoring cell within Ministry of Chemicals to track 6 critical items weekly.

**Structure**:
- Head: DCPC/Secretary-level ownership
- Members: Representatives from DGFT, Ministry of Textiles, company IRS
- Tools: Weekly checklist (provided), quarterly dashboard, escalation protocols
- Frequency: Weekly status, monthly reports to Minister

**Cost**: ₹1-2 crore annual (staff, tools); benefit = early warning for risk mitigation.
