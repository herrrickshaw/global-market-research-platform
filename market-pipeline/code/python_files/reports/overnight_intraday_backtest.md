# Overnight vs intraday tug of war — replication on US/JP/KR/IN/EU

Replicates Lou, Polk & Skouras (2019), *A tug of war: overnight versus intraday expected returns*, JFE 134(1) — Table I (ON/ID sorts), Table II (MOM, STR) and Appendix Table A2 (international). Generated by `backtest_overnight_intraday.py`.

Holding months: 2016-02 – 2026-08. Cells: mean monthly winner-minus-loser (decile 10 − 1) return, Newey-West(3) t-stat in parentheses. STR is low-minus-high. ALL (avg) = equal-weight average across markets of each month's spread.

**Paper benchmarks (US 1993–2013, VW, excess):** ON sort → overnight +3.47% (t 16.6), intraday −3.24% (t −9.3). ID sort → intraday +2.19% (t 6.7), overnight −1.81% (t −8.4). Nine non-US markets pooled: ON overnight +2.31% (t 6.9), ID intraday +2.80% (t 6.2), cross-period reversal of similar size. MOM (12-2): overnight CAPM alpha +0.98% (t 3.8), intraday −0.02% (t −0.1). STR (low − high): no close-to-close effect; overnight +0.93% (t 4.3), intraday −1.05% (t −3.3).

## Deviations from the paper

- Open is the exchange daily open, not the 9:30–10:00 VWAP (paper footnote 8 reports robustness to CRSP opens). Flat opens set the overnight leg to zero.
- No market cap: weights are formation-month median turnover normalised within exchange suffix; breakpoints are own-market quantiles; the microcap screen is the bottom 30% by turnover (plus a $5 floor in US).
- Raw long-short spreads only — no CAPM/FF3 adjustment, no transaction costs.
- Stock-months with a price break (close or open outside 0.5×–2× prior close) are dropped in both sort and holding month; the holding-month filter uses future data.
- ~10 years (2016–2026) vs the paper's 21; EU is a pooled multi-exchange panel on raw (unadjusted) prices.
