## Three Pathways to Savings

### 1️⃣ **PETROCHEMICAL FEEDSTOCKS** (Textiles) — ₹50,000–95,000 cr/year

India faces **$20.4bn annual feedstock import deficit** (polymers + chemicals). Government capex of **$38.5bn** (BPCL AP ₹1L cr, RIL O2C ₹75k cr, L&T Bina ₹5-10k cr, BHAVYA Parks ₹3,030 cr) unlocks domestic capacity.

**Capacity Roadmap (FY25→FY30)**:
- Polypropylene: 2.3→4.1 MMTPA (+78%) — **import dependency eliminated**
- Polyethylene: 2.1→4.2 MMTPA (+100%) — 70-80% of gap closed
- Polyester intermediates: New capacity — 30-40% domestic sourcing

**Cost Savings**:
- INDUTECH: $150-200/MT (BPCL Bina LLDPE) → ₹19,920 cr/year
- HOMETECH: $120-150/MT → ₹5,478 cr/year
- BUILDTECH + MOBILTECH: $100-170/MT → ₹9,379 cr/year
- PACKTECH: Export-led growth → ₹2,241 cr/year + ₹13,280 cr specialty segments
- **Subtotal: ₹50,298–95,450 cr/year** (base to optimistic)

**Export Competitiveness**: 2-4% cost advantage vs Bangladesh/Vietnam/Indonesia by FY30

---

### 2️⃣ **ETHANOL BLENDING & PETROL EXPORT** — ₹12,000–18,000 cr/year

Ethanol displaces domestic petrol; vehicles buy more litres for same km (4-7% mileage loss). Freed petrol is India's largest export earner in petroleum products.

**Benefit Streams (E20→E30 ramp FY26-FY30)**:

| Item | FY30 Value (E27 base) |
|------|---|
| **Freed petrol export earnings** | ₹62,300 cr ($7.3bn) |
| **OMC pump throughput margin** | ₹1,000 cr |
| **Tax revenue (excise + VAT)** | ₹11,500 cr |
| **State SGST on ethanol (price-neutral)** | ₹4,787 cr ← policy enabler |
| **Total** | **₹79,587 crores/year** |

**Supply Constraint**: Ethanol capacity = 4,530 cr L/year planned (overcapacity exists). Feedstock (maize, FCI rice leg) is the binding limit.

**YoY Average (FY26-FY30)**: ₹12,000–18,000 cr as blend ramps E20→E30

---

### 3️⃣ **RENEWABLE FUELS & GAS SUBSTITUTION** — ₹10,000–14,000 cr/year

**CBG (Compressed Biogas)** via SATAT programme + policy stack displaces LNG imports.

**FY30 Target**: 5 MMTPA CBG (SATAT scale-up from 0.5 MMTPA today)

**LNG Import Displacement**:
- Current LNG imports: ~180 MMTPA gas equivalent, ₹80,000 cr/year cost
- CBG displacement by FY30: 10-15% (18 MMTPA) = **₹8,000–12,000 crores saved**

**Secondary Opportunities**:
- Bio-isobutanol (SAF route): ₹2,000–3,000 cr/year (niche, high-margin)
- Distillery conversion (grain-based ethanol): ₹500–1,000 cr/year

**YoY Benefit (FY30)**: ₹10,000–14,000 crores/year

- [ ] **Ethanol SGST Policy**: Draft state-level 5% SGST on ethanol (price-neutral design)

- [ ] **BPCL AP commissioning**: Ethylene/propylene crackers online (Q3-FY30 target)

- [ ] **Anti-dumping preparation**: If China polyester dumping accelerates
- [ ] **Export marketing**: Launch "Made in India Textiles" campaign leveraging cost advantage

## Key Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|---|
| Refinery capex delays (BPCL/RIL) | Allocate ₹5,000 cr risk capital; phase capex; fast-track approvals |
| Crude < $60/bbl (substitution uneconomical) | Hedging instruments; floor pricing guardrails; blend anchor |
| Ethanol feedstock scarcity | Diversify (maize ↔ FCI rice ↔ molasses ↔ stalk); waste valorization |
| China polyester dumping | Monitor 2027-28; anti-dumping duties if domestic capacity idle |
| Private sector underinvestment | BHAVYA/PM Mitra subsidy structure (₹3,030 cr + 20-25% capex support) |
| State revenue resistance | State SGST design (₹4,787 cr for E27 @ 5%, price-neutral) |

## Summary

**Three interconnected import substitution pathways unlock ₹75,000–95,000 crores of annual savings by FY30**, contingent on:

1. **₹1,38,514 cr (~$16.6bn) government + private sector capex** (BPCL AP, RIL O2C, L&T Bina, BHAVYA, PM Mitra, SATAT)
2. **Policy consistency**: Ethanol SGST design, import duty escalation, SATAT offtake guarantee, anti-dumping vigilance
3. **Execution discipline**: BPCL AP cracker by Q3-FY27 is critical path; RIL O2C ramp by FY29; ethanol feedstock management

**Realistic confidence**: 70% execution → ₹75,000–90,000 cr/year benefit (HIGH confidence)  
**Upside**: All capex on-time + policy aligned → ₹95,000–130,000 cr/year (MEDIUM confidence)
