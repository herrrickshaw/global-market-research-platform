## STRATEGIC IMPORT SUBSTITUTION RECOMMENDATIONS

### High Priority: Incentivize Domestic Producers

**Tier 1 Chemicals (Capex-Triggered)**:
1. **Reliance Industries** — Expand Jamnagar + Vadodara (ethylene, PP, PE, TPA)
2. **BPCL** — AP greenfield cracker (ethylene, propylene, PP)
3. **IOCL** — Panipat capacity expansion (ethylene, PP, TPA)
4. **L&T-BPCL Bina JV** — LLDPE swing unit (2026–28)

**Policy Levers**:
- PLI Scheme expansion (chemicals focus, not just electronics/autos)
- Customs duty escalation (10% → 20%, phased FY26–28) on P1 chemicals
- R&D grants for specialty chemical R&D (dyes, pigments, auxiliaries)

---

### Medium Priority: Emerging Domestic Players

**Tier 2 Chemicals (Dependent)**:
1. **Jindal Polyester** — Expand TPA, PET capacity (currently 500 KTPA)
2. **Deepak Nitrite** — Azo dyes, pigments (leverage existing capex)
3. **Kiri Industries** — Export-led dye production (potential for domestic uptake)
4. **Excel Industries** — Acetic acid expansion (currently 250 KTPA, constrained by feedstock)

**Policy Levers**:
- BHAVYA Rasayan Parks (3 sites) for specialty chemicals (dyes, pigments, auxiliaries)
- Environmental clearance fast-track for capex projects
- State-level SGST waivers for export-competitive producers

---

### Quick Wins: Oilseed-Based Chemicals

**Tier 2.5 (Immediate)**:
1. **Cargill India** — Lecithin extraction from soya (minimal capex, high ROI)
2. **Bunge India** — Rapeseed, sunflower lecithin (underutilized capacity)
3. **Oleochemical pathway** — Castor oil → textile auxiliaries (emerging, R&D needed)

**Policy Levers**:
- Direct incentives for oilseed processors to add lecithin extraction units (₹50–100 cr capex)
- MSP support for oilseeds (soya, rapeseed, sunflower) to boost domestic feedstock
- Export duty on oilseeds (to protect domestic processing industry)
