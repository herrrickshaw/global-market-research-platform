🎯 Strategic Recommendations

1. Refined Petroleum Export Expansion (Asia-Focused)

Target: Shift +500K bbl/day to Asia (net +$2.7B/yr)

  • Focus markets: India (+4% YoY petroleum demand), Southeast Asia (refinery shortfalls)
  • Product priority: Gasoline (HS 271012) and Diesel (HS 271019) — premium margins
  • Best routes: Port of Houston (3.5M bbl/day capacity) → Asia (7,500 nm)
  • Expected margins: $12-15/bbl net (after $3.20/bbl logistics)
  • Timeline: Q3-Q4 2026 with 12-month voyage cycles
2. LNG Terminal Expansion (Highest Value Growth)

Target: Expand from 12 to 15 BCF/day (+$12B/yr opportunity)

  • Primary lever: Freeport expansion (+1.5 BCF/day by 2027) = +$4.8B/yr alone
  • Target buyers: Japan (20% global LNG), South Korea, India (growing +8% YoY)
  • Operating terminals: Sabine Pass (3.8 BCF/day ✅), Freeport (2.1+1.5 BCF/day), Corpus Christi (1.4 BCF/day)
  • Expected margins: $1.70/MMBtu net to Asia (after $2.50/MMBtu shipping)
  • Constraint: Henry Hub pricing vs. global benchmark — monitor spreads
3. Thermal Coal Market Growth (India Opportunity)

Target: Increase from 50M to 75M tons/year (+$425M/yr)

  • Target market: India (imports 75% of coal needs, +3.2% YoY, 70% of electricity is coal-based)
  • Route optimization: Shift to Pacific ports (LA, Long Beach) for faster Asia transit vs Gulf
  • Expected margins: $17/ton net to Asia (after $28/ton freight) vs. -$7/ton loss to Europe
  • Competitive advantage: Avoid European green transition pressure; focus South Asia
  • Timeline: 2-3 year ramp-up to capture growing demand
4. Port Infrastructure & Logistics Optimization

Port allocation strategy:

  • Port of Houston (98/100): Primary hub for refined + LNG + coke. Maximize 90% utilization.
  • Port of Beaumont (85/100): Specialize in refined petroleum. Secondary for LNG spillover.
  • Port of Los Angeles/Long Beach (75/100): Shift coal exports from Gulf to Pacific for Asia advantage.
  • Terminal capacity: All 5 LNG terminals at 100% utilization; prioritize new expansions (Freeport).

📐 Methodology & Data Sources

Scoring Model (100-Point Scale)

Factor Weight Scoring Criteria
Market Position 30 pts Global market share (13%+ = 30 pts, 6-8% = 20 pts, Low = 10 pts)
Price Level 25 pts Commodity price & margin opportunity (High $50+ = 25, Medium = 15, Low = 5)
Port Infrastructure 20 pts Dedicated port capacity & specialization (3+ ports = 20, 1-2 = 15, Limited = 5)
Transport Mode 15 pts Logistics efficiency (Tanker/Bulk optimized = 15, Bulk = 14, Container = 10)
Export Reach 10 pts Multi-region demand & liquidity (Multi-region = 8-10, Single = 5)

Data Sources

  • EIA API: Energy Information Administration (crude, LNG, natural gas, coal prices)
  • FRED API: Federal Reserve Economic Data (commodity price indices)
  • US Census Bureau: Trade Data by commodity & port
  • Port authorities: Houston, Corpus Christi, Los Angeles, Long Beach (capacity data)
  • Market data: 2024 YTD actual + Aug 2026 forecasted

Key Assumptions

  • Margins calculated: Gross commodity price - Average logistics costs by route
  • LNG export costs include liquefaction, shipping, regasification (~2-3 $/MMBtu)
  • Coal freight to Asia: ~$28/ton via Pacific, ~$42/ton via Gulf + transshipment
  • Refined product margins: Exclude distribution network costs; bulk tanker shipping only
  • No tariff duties applied (all HS codes 0% duty under USMCA)
