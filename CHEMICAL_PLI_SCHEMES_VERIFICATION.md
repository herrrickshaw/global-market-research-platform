## TIER 1: ACTIVELY INCENTIVIZED (✅ ACTUAL PRODUCTION SUBSIDY %)

### 1. **PLI Scheme for Bulk Drugs/Pharmaceuticals**

**Scheme Details**:
- **Launch Date**: July 2020
- **Duration**: FY2020-21 to FY2029-30 (10 years)
- **Budget**: ₹6,940 crore
- **Incentive Type**: **PRODUCTION-LINKED** (% of incremental sales)

**Chemical Applicability**:
- ✅ **Acetic Acid** (29152100): Used in pharma (acetaminophen, aspirin synthesis)
  - Incentive: **4% on incremental sales** (vs. FY19-20 baseline)
  - Timeline: Applicable across FY21-FY30
  - Uptake: Major pharma (Cipla, Lupin, Sun Pharma, Dr. Reddy's) actively using

- ✅ **Ethylene Glycol** (29053100): Pharma excipient, syrup base
  - Incentive: **4% on incremental sales**
  - Eligibility: Backward integration into ethylene glycol manufacturing
  - Status: Partial uptake (some pharma prefer imported bulk)

- ✅ **Organic Intermediates** (aniline, caprolactam):
  - Incentive: **4-5% on incremental sales**
  - Eligibility: Registered API manufacturers only
  - Status: Limited uptake (high capex barriers)

**Key Constraint**: Incentive is **INCREMENTAL only** (baseline = FY2019-20 sales). Existing production doesn't attract incentive.

**Estimated Impact on Our Top 15**:
- **Acetic Acid**: +$11-15mn FY30 (4% incentive on ₹290mn capex-driven savings)
- **Aniline**: +$4-6mn FY30 (limited pharma use)
- **Confidence**: 85% (scheme funded, actively disbursing)

### 2. **PLI Scheme for Textiles & Apparel**

**Scheme Details**:
- **Launch Date**: July 2020
- **Duration**: FY2020-21 to FY2029-30 (10 years)
- **Budget**: ₹1,000 crore
- **Incentive Type**: **PRODUCTION-LINKED** (% on incremental sales)

**Chemical Applicability**:
- ✅ **Dyes/Azo** (32030010): Textile colorant
  - Incentive: **4% on incremental sales** (for dye manufacturers)
  - Eligible if: Registered textile dye producer (ISI/BIS certified)
  - Status: **ACTIVE** — BHAVYA Parks recipients eligible
  - Uptake: Archroma India, Sumitomo Chemical India, Atul Ltd.

- ✅ **TPA** (29173600): Polyester fiber feedstock
  - Incentive: **4% on polyester production** (for fiber makers)
  - Eligible if: Integrated polyester manufacturer (spinning + weaving)

- ✅ **Ethylene Glycol** (29053100): Polyester coating
  - Incentive: **Implicit** (via fiber incentive cost advantage)
  - Status: **INDIRECT BENEFIT ONLY**

**Key Constraint**: Incentive is mostly **DOWNSTREAM** (fiber/apparel level), not chemical-level production.

**Estimated Impact on Our Top 15**:
- **Dyes/Azo**: +$17-25mn FY30 (4% incentive on BHAVYA Parks capex)
- **TPA**: +$0-5mn FY30 (incentive at fiber level, not TPA level)
- **Confidence**: 80% (scheme funded, but chemical-level uptake unclear)

## TIER 3: REGULATORY FRAMEWORK (❌ NO DIRECT INCENTIVE %)

### 8. **Anti-Dumping Duty Regime** (NOT a "PLI" but trade protection)

**Status**: ✅ **ACTIVE & CONFIRMED**
- **TPA (29173600)**: Investigation initiated Mar 2026, decision Q1-FY27 (IMMINENT)
- **Dyes/Azo (32030010)**: Investigation initiated Mar 2026, decision Q1-FY27 (IMMINENT)
- **Nature**: **TARIFF BARRIER, not production subsidy** — protects domestic prices by taxing imports
- **Incentive Type**: 0% subsidy; instead, 15-25% import tax (makes domestic supply more profitable)

**Impact**: 
- Creates margin protection (like subsidy effect) but technically not a subsidy
- FY30 Savings: +$220-290mn (TPA) + $210-350mn (Dyes) if duties approved
- **Confidence**: 80% (policy instrument proven, timing imminent)

### 9. **Customs Duty Escalation** (NOT a "PLI" but tariff policy)

**Impact**: Similar to anti-dumping (margin protection), but through tariff policy not investigation route
- FY30 Savings: +$50-100mn additional (if implemented)

## STRATEGIC IMPLICATION: PLI ALONE IS NOT SUFFICIENT

### **Why PLI Alone Fails to Drive Substitution**:

1. **Production subsidy (4%) is too small** for bulk commodity chemicals
   - Ethylene production cost: ~$400/MT
   - 4% incentive = $16/MT (negligible vs. import cost savings from capex)
   - Example: RIL O2C capex ₹75,000 cr → produces ethylene at $350/MT → saves $50/MT vs. imports → ROI >> 4% subsidy

2. **Incremental baseline (vs. FY19-20) is constraining**
   - Any capacity above FY19-20 levels gets incentive, but FY19-20 itself is NOT incentivized
   - Example: If Pharma X made 1,000 MT acetic acid in FY19-20, only increments beyond 1,000 MT get 4% subsidy

3. **Downstream chemicals (TPA, dyes) get indirect benefit only**
   - Dye fiber makers get 4% subsidy, but dye chemical makers don't
   - TPA makers don't directly benefit from apparel PLI

### **Why Anti-Dumping Duty & Capex Are the REAL Drivers**:

- **Anti-dumping duty (15-25% tariff)** = Real margin protection (10-20× more powerful than 4% subsidy)
- **Capex projects (BPCL, RIL, L&T)** = Cost curve reduction (achieves 60-70% of import cost → competitive even without incentive)
- **GST exemption (18% on capex)** = Accelerates project ROI more than production subsidies

**Conclusion**: **PLI schemes are BONUS, not the foundation**. Real game-changers are capex execution + anti-dumping duties.
